📜 How to play

Capture more enemy dots than your opponent. Only captured dots score!

1

You and your opponent take turns placing one dot

2

Surround an enemy dot with a chain of yours — diagonals count

3

The dot inside is captured: +1 point. More dots inside = more points

  • Dots on the board edge can never be captured
  • You can free your captured dots by surrounding the enemy chain around them
  • The battle ends when one side can no longer catch up

📱 Controls

Press, aim, release to place a dot. Two fingers: move and zoom the board.
