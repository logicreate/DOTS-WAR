📜 DOTS WAR — How to Play

Surround enemy dots to capture territory. Most captures wins!

1

Take turns placing dots on the grid crossings

2

Close a loop of your dots around enemy dots

3

Everything inside is yours — each captured dot = 1 point

📱 Controls

Touch: press, aim (a magnifier appears), release to place. Two fingers: move & zoom the board

⚔️ AI Levels

  • 🟢 Rookie — random, great for kids
  • 🔵 Warrior — sees your captures and blocks them — but sometimes slips
  • 🔴 Legend — reads many moves ahead, sets traps and builds walls

🌐 Online

  • Create a room → share the 6-digit code
  • Friend enters code → battle begins!
